Two Ways I Can Help

Whether you need an online presence or you're tired of running your business on WhatsApp groups and spreadsheets, here's what I offer.

Company Profile Website

A professional website for your business. Shows who you are, what you offer, and makes it easy for people to reach you. Ideal if you don't have a website yet, or your current one looks outdated and isn't bringing in new customers.

  • Home, about, and services or product pages built around your business, not a generic template
  • Photo gallery, pricing, and a contact form or direct WhatsApp button
  • Looks good and works properly on phones, since that's how most customers will find you

Custom Application / System

If managing your business by WhatsApp, Excel, or paper is starting to slow you down, I can build a system made specifically for how you operate. A few common examples:

  • Cashier / POS for shops, cafes, and restaurants
  • Booking / reservation so customers can pick their own schedule
  • HR & payroll for attendance, leave, and salary calculation
  • Manufacturing / production (ERP) to track orders, stock, and production in one place
  • Customer management (CRM) so no lead or follow-up gets forgotten
  • WhatsApp / email blast to reach many customers at once
  • ...and other systems, depending on what your business actually needs
